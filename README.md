### 🛒 Current Project — SmartCart

<table>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <h3>⚡ SmartCart-v1 <small><code>Actively Building • Current Project</code></small></h3>
      <p><strong>Smart inventory, billing and stock management system for small grocery and retail businesses.</strong></p>
      <p>SmartCart is engineered to streamline business operations for local store owners, replacing manual ledgers with an intuitive Android application that links sales directly to live stock data.</p>
      <p><strong>Key Features:</strong></p>
      <ul>
        <li>📦 <strong>Product Management:</strong> Cataloging, editing, and managing retail item inventory.</li>
        <li>📊 <strong>Inventory Tracking:</strong> Live monitoring of current stock levels and updates.</li>
        <li>🧾 <strong>Billing:</strong> Fast point-of-sale checkout and receipt computation.</li>
        <li>⚡ <strong>Automatic Stock Deduction:</strong> Instant database quantity subtraction upon billing.</li>
        <li>⚠️ <strong>Low-Stock Detection:</strong> Real-time alerts for replenishment needs.</li>
        <li>📈 <strong>Inventory Analytics:</strong> Practical visual metrics on store inventory.</li>
        <li>🔍 <strong>Search &amp; Filter:</strong> Quick item lookup by name, category, or code.</li>
        <li>🔐 <strong>Authentication:</strong> Secure login with Firebase Authentication.</li>
        <li>🖥️ <strong>Dashboard &amp; Settings:</strong> Intuitive administrative control and store preferences.</li>
      </ul>
      <p><strong>Technology Stack:</strong> <code>Java</code> • <code>Android Studio</code> • <code>Firebase Auth</code> • <code>Firebase Firestore</code> • <code>Material Design 3</code> • <code>Git &amp; GitHub</code></p>
